# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful, constructive, and accessible.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and discuss ideas without attacking people.
- Give useful, specific feedback and accept that people may disagree.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information without permission.
- Keep project discussions focused and make room for others to participate.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Publishing private information, or encouraging others to do so.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or project services.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, and other spaces used for this project.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. They will consider the impact and context of each incident; repeated or severe behavior may result in a permanent ban.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately using the contact options on the [project owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/AlphaBeast97). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
